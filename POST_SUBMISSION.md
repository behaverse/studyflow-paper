# Post-submission parking lot

Until Fri 2026-10-16, new ideas land here instead of in the paper. After submission, this list feeds the revision (a Reviewed Preprint can be revised) and the next paper.

One line per idea: date, idea, and why the submission does not need it.

- 2026-09-26: More tasks in the example study. They add operations, panels, and prose, and make nothing more testable.
- 2026-09-30: A language model drafts the encodings of published studies, and the validator's errors on its drafts are counted. The two encodings made by hand already show what a Methods section leaves unstated, and the paper names this measurement as future work.
- 2026-09-30: A comprehension study of Studyflow figures against the methods paragraphs they replace. The paper claims no readability gain (its Limitations call it untested), and the study needs human participants and ethics approval.
- 2026-09-30: A conformance suite shared by independent runtimes. It waits for the move of both runtimes onto one engine and for a second, independent runtime; the paper says their agreement rests on shared fixtures, not a suite.
- 2026-09-30: A human cohort taking the same file. The paper states it as a design goal it does not demonstrate and verifies the file on simulated participants; a human run needs a runtime that a person can take and that executes the full semantics, plus recruitment and ethics approval.
