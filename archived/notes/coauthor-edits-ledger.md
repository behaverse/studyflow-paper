## 3. Style rules his edits imply, to apply across the paper

- **Signpost and conclude.** Open sections with what is coming ("Here we present", "The key insight …"), and close them with a concluding sentence ("In summary", "therefore addresses …").
- **Explain every technical term once, plainly, or point to the glossary.** His comments on "ISO-standardized", XML, "scheme" and "library" all asked for this.
- **Say what something shows in this study, not what the feature does in general.** His comment on the views paragraph: "say something about what those views show about your specific study".
- **One word, one meaning.** He flagged step vs operation, fragment vs stage, draw (depict vs sample), projection vs view, and structure vs topology.
- **Simplify complex sentences**, and say why something matters ("reproducibility, quality control").
- **Use "e.g." and "i.e." parentheticals and colon-led elaborations**, his habitual way of making claims concrete.
- **Be honest about the demonstration's status:** "current capabilities", "for convenience", "we also discuss future directions".
